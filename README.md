# 🩺 Smart IoT-Based Health Monitoring & Automatic Medicine Distribution System

## 🌍 Overview
Our project introduces a **wearable IoT-based health monitoring system** that continuously tracks key biomarkers — such as **heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO₂), ECG, temperature, and blood pressure** — in real time.  
The system goes beyond monitoring by integrating with an **automatic medicine distribution unit**, enabling precise, scheduled, and condition-triggered drug delivery without human intervention.

By combining **real-time health sensing**, **data-driven decision-making**, and **automated treatment**, this platform aims to enhance patient safety, reduce medical risks, and deliver personalized healthcare support.

---

## ⚙️ Core Components

### 🧠 1. Smart Wearable Patch
A lightweight, multi-sensor device continuously monitoring patient vitals:
- **ECG** – Heart rhythm tracking  
- **SpO₂** – Oxygen saturation  
- **Body Temperature** – Fever detection  
- **Blood Pressure** – Circulatory health analysis  

Data is transmitted securely to the backend for real-time analysis.

---

### 💊 2. Automatic Medicine Distribution Unit
An **IoT-enabled dispenser** that automatically provides pills or liquid medication (e.g., insulin) based on:
- Sensor-triggered alerts (e.g., low oxygen, high BP)
- Scheduled dosage times
- Doctor-configured prescriptions

It verifies intake using sensors and syncs all logs with the cloud, ensuring accuracy, traceability, and patient compliance.

---

### 🧬 3. Digital Twin of the Patient
A **virtual replica** of each patient that simulates their vitals, medical history, and lifestyle.  
This twin helps in:
- Predicting treatment outcomes  
- Testing dosage changes without real-world risk  
- Supporting doctors with personalized insights  

---

### 🤖 4. Machine Learning & Predictive Analytics
The backend leverages **machine learning** models trained on historical patient data to:
- Predict potential health anomalies  
- Optimize medication schedules  
- Enable preventive healthcare decisions
